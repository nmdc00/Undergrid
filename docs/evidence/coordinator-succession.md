# Current unanimous coordinator succession

TDD §7.3 requires an explicit successor proposal, unanimous currently required contributor/acceptor consent and the nominated eligible agent's separate acceptance. Coordinator disappearance does not grant an administrator, observer or silence authority to appoint a successor. A required absent party can block succession legitimately; permitted-export forking remains the documented exit.
