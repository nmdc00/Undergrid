# Prompt refresh of an already scanned intention watch

TDD §§5.4/6; implementation `cd02dd5`, no schema migration. This is a network worker scheduling correction, not a new semantic matching/provider qualification claim.

## Observed defect and behavior

The full release `7b645fb` network race gate failed after 1047.626s on the previously scanned watcher assertion. Persisted opportunity creation was 7.130s after the later candidate's creation, exceeding the existing five-second contract. Root's focused retained-fixture reproduction also failed after 15.689s. The prior foreground job scanned the newly committed source only; previously scanned watchers depended on a global repair lane that serially visited up to 1000 watches. Its coalesced global timestamp reset could also couple unrelated row locks.

Committed intention changes now atomically enqueue a candidate refresh in addition to the original cause-specific discovery and coalesced repair jobs. A dedicated `intention_watch_refresh` pair queue has two slots, while `intention_watch_fanout` has one independent slot. Both fetch at a bounded 100ms cooldown/poll interval; fanout continuations cannot wait behind their own pair jobs. Previously committed fanout jobs in the pair queue remain supported. A fanout job reads at most 100 current watchers, compares each with the current approved public candidate through the existing single `CompareDemand` policy, and transactionally inserts only compatible pair jobs plus a durable cursor continuation. Pair jobs have priority 1; fanout/continuation jobs priority 2. There is no synchronous request scan and no private candidate projection is published.

Each pair reuses the existing scan transaction, restricted to its exact candidate. Current source principal, agent, active watch, expiry, revision and candidate public eligibility are rechecked. Owner locks retain principal→agent→intention order. Urgent lock waits are bounded to 100ms; lock contention snoozes the durable job 250ms without exhausting normal failure attempts. A short wait behind ordinary HTTP reads avoids polling starvation; long unrelated owner holds cannot occupy an urgent worker indefinitely. The pair does not advance a full-scan clock, because matching one candidate does not prove all candidates were scanned.

Modern coalesced refresh remains catchup over independently committed watches, without globally resetting/locking every watch row. Periodic due repair stays independent. Previously persisted default-queue jobs retain their original full watcher-refresh/sweep/recruitment effects. Running/delayed/failed refresh jobs still cannot absorb a new committed command's immediate signal. River now serves eight queues with a 16-connection maintenance/fetch pool; the foreground discovery and repair/recruitment isolation remains intact.

## Pragmatic assessment and limits

The compatibility rule and normalized opportunity/notification transaction are shared rather than copied into a new matcher. Fanout is a bounded, read-only eligibility filter; effect-bearing pairs independently recheck current state under the existing owner locks. Cursor continuation and child enqueue commit together, while unique opportunity input hashes dedupe retries and notification effects. Job payloads carry IDs/cursors, not agent executable content. Connections, transactions and rows have explicit cleanup; unexpected database errors propagate rather than silently dropping work.

This proves the selected bounded scheduling, lock-recovery and privacy seams on fresh/retained local fixtures. It does not prove arbitrary-load SLA, external provider/source qualification, beneficial-owner independence, full TDD completion, main/develop promotion or production deployment. No financial database, credential or deployment identity is changed.
