# Coordinator succession implementation contract

TDD §7.3 permits existing principals to propose a successor after the coordinator disappears, with unanimous currently required contributor/acceptor approval. Succession changes governance only: it never accepts an assignment, changes plan/rights, marks work complete, grants private history to an outsider or revokes an existing license. Silence and timeout are not consent. A required absent party can legitimately prevent succession; the documented permitted-export fork remains the exit rather than an administrative bypass.

Entrypoints are `POST /v1/swarms/{id}/coordinator-proposals`, `GET /v1/coordinator-proposals/{id}`, and versioned `POST /v1/coordinator-proposals/{id}/approvals` and `/accept`. A current accepted member with explicit proposal scope may propose an eligible accepted contributor as successor. Proposal alone grants no vote or appointment authority. Exact-hash principal votes and the nominated agent's separate explicit acceptance are required. Proposal creation binds the source room version; subsequent proposal actions bind the immutable proposal revision and require Idempotency-Key. Replays recover their original authorized result without repeating events, while changed payload/precondition conflicts. Current membership and credential authority apply even on replay.

The electorate is the accepted plan's exact required contributor and acceptance-principal sets, with current accepted membership checked, plus the successor principal and every currently binding task principal even when a different accepted plan is active. Without an accepted plan, accepted contributor-role principals plus the successor principal are required. Existing plan contracts require acceptance principals to be required non-observer contributors; an observer is not silently promoted to a buyer/acceptor governance voter. An old coordinator with separately evidenced current contribution/task obligations remains required; only holding the office alone is excluded. Observers may propose with explicit scope but cannot vote or appoint unless they are a current required party under the governing contract. The companion documents supply no different succession rule. Holding the coordinator office alone does not create a veto, but an old coordinator named as a required plan party cannot be removed from consent. Membership identity/role/version, accepted-plan hash/head, relevant policy hashes/versions and successor identity are snapshot inputs. General room sequence/version is not a vote snapshot input: ordinary messages and appended votes must not invalidate otherwise current consent. Changes invalidate the proposal rather than silently recomputing its approvers. Deadlines expire pending proposals without transferring authority.

New explicit `coordinator:propose`, `coordinator:approve` and `coordinator:accept` scopes are explicit; approval/acceptance additionally require an explicit live owner policy opt-in for coordinator succession. Existing rights-template acceptance does not implicitly authorize governance. Approval and acceptance remain separate even when the same principal controls several agents. Each required principal votes once, and the nominated exact agent must accept.

The source transition must obey the recruitment guard contract: first identify the real current credential and verify current actor membership/room visibility, then before actor/room locks acquire the bounded relevant current room authority principal rows in UUID order, followed by agent rows in UUID order. The ordered guard rejects more than500 accepted memberships with `409 succession_roster_limit`; it never truncates an electorate or claims partial unanimity. This finite lock-budget limit requires further qualification before increasing it. Recheck current coordinator and snapshot under the room lock; transfer updates coordinator and the old/new membership roles/versions atomically (an office-only predecessor becomes an observer; a separately required contributor retains contributor status) with proposal state, votes, events and inbox work. No source swarm lock may be introduced after a recipient authority lock in recruitment. The pre-auth helper must expose no room metadata or lock arbitrary foreign rooms for an unauthorized caller. A coordinator identity change while acquiring guards fails/retries instead of proceeding under stale locks.

Network migration027 is reserved if normalized proposal/vote persistence is required. Existing plan approvals cannot represent this distinct purpose by fabricating a plan or attaching succession consent to a rights grant. Public REST contract, finite MCP/A2A command registry and UI will expose the actual supported workflow after its domain proof. Archive, assignment replacement and source-policy changes are separate features.

Implementation and actual restricted-login PostgreSQL evidence are recorded in [the succession evidence](evidence/coordinator-succession.md). This contract and a scoped passing test do not establish full-TDD, release, provider or deployment qualification.
